Connection settings

Use a TURN service with temporary session credentials. For restricted networks, choose TURNS over TCP on port 443.

Never paste an account API key or account password. These relay credentials are shared with students through the session link. Use short-lived, limited credentials that cover the session. They are kept only in this tab and participating student tabs, and are not saved to GitHub.

Privacy and connection information

Drawings and explanations stay in browser memory and travel to the tutor through encrypted WebRTC connections. In TURN relay mode, encrypted traffic passes through your selected relay provider. No artwork is stored in an app database or uploaded to GitHub. PeerJS Cloud handles connection signalling, and Google STUN helps direct-mode devices connect. These providers, the relay provider and the website host process connection information, including IP addresses. Relay provider logging and retention depend on the service you choose. Peer connections also expose network addresses to the other connected device. No analytics, cookies or browser storage are used by this app.

All active tabs clear the activity after 90 minutes, or when they receive “End and delete”. A disconnected or suspended device cannot be remotely erased; it clears on expiry when its page next runs. Screenshots and browser or device recovery are outside the app’s control.

TURN relay mode forces submissions through the configured relay so you can test it explicitly. Direct mode uses the original connection method without a relay. Some university networks block WebRTC or PeerJS signalling entirely. Test a student submission on the teaching network before starting the activity; a successful relay reachability test alone does not prove submissions work.
